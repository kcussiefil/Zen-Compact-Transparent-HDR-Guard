# Zen Compact Transparent HDR Guard v0.3.0 — Glance compositor recovery and faster video-tab switching

## Changes

- Reworked the **default-OFF** Zen Glance glass option on macOS. The temporary composition hint is now restricted to selected Glance foreground/background containers and is suppressed while any visible Zia or Site Folders preview card takes over the compositing role. This avoids layering our Glance promotion on top of Zia's hover-card promotion.
- Added an event-driven `glance-recovery.uc.js` guard. After Glance closes it briefly releases the selected normal-page compositor for one animation frame, then removes the marker. It does not run on a detected video page, an active `browser[zia-video]`, during a Glance session, or while the preference is off. No always-on compositor force or frequent polling was added.
- Returning to an already verified video tab no longer flushes the full actor state cache; a same-browser/same-URL SDR/HDR classification can be rendered immediately. A new URL, YouTube SPA video navigation, same-URL reload or tab close invalidates the relevant browser's saved records, without resetting other tabs.
- Reduced unknown manually allowed SDR-site fallback from **350 ms to 100 ms**. Confirmed stream evidence still wins, and unknown/non-whitelisted sites remain protected.
- Updated Traditional Chinese Sine labels: `需 Zia 的 Blur over video 開啟`, and removed the unnecessary `macOS 測試功能` wording from the Glance option. No user-facing preferences or their default values were otherwise changed.

## SDR/HDR and Zia compatibility

- Same-tab caching never turns the *monitor* being in HDR mode into proof that the *video stream* is HDR. It caches actor-supplied stream evidence for unchanged documents only. Confirmed HDR wins over site/manual SDR rules; unknown remains conservative unless the explicit SDR whitelist fallback is enabled.
- **Bilibili** site-wide immediate custom glass remains unchanged. This user-enabled exception intentionally bypasses SDR/HDR checks and may display the wrong appearance for an HDR video.
- Zia remains the owner of normal preview-card compositor hints and video blur policy. The Glance option is still default-OFF, and a detected video prevents the new post-close recomposition step. Existing Site Folders groups, visual layout and on-screen colors are not intentionally modified.
- Zia `Blur over video` and the separate HDR custom-glass option can still make HDR footage **dim or washed out** on macOS due to the underlying browser compositor. A correct-looking screenshot does not demonstrate EDR/HDR preservation.

## Validation and hardware limitations

- The draft branch adds Node.js tests for per-browser state reuse, changed URL and reload invalidation, the 100 ms fallback, a closed-tab cache discard, the Glance-only compositor conditions, and the one-frame non-video recovery. Existing legacy regressions, JavaScript syntax checks and release-policy checks are re-run by GitHub CI before merging.
- **Actual macOS OLED HDR/SDR hardware testing was not performed** by these automated checks. The workaround targets the observed loss of glass after a Glance preview, but GPU layer invalidation cannot be certified without on-device tests. Validate normal, SDR and HDR video pages, Glance open/close, Site Folders and Zia hover cards, and repeated YouTube tab switches. If a persistent blur problem survives after Glance closes, use the rollback below and restart Zen.

## Rollback

- Immediate isolated mitigation: turn **OFF** the `mod.compacttpr.glanceglass` setting in Sine and restart Zen. This removes the additional Glance compositor and recovery paths, leaving the other HDR Guard settings alone.
- To revert both v0.3.0 changes, install previous tagged release **v0.2.9** (https://github.com/kcussiefil/Zen-Compact-Transparent-HDR-Guard/releases/tag/v0.2.9). Its full commit SHA is `ef7d4f9510f80b3cadb97e3ccdabae714e6c2689`.
- The backup branch **`backup/v0.2.9-before-v0.3.0`** points to `ef7d4f9510f80b3cadb97e3ccdabae714e6c2689`; do not delete it.
