# HDR Guard v0.2.3 — Optional Zia URL/search pop-up custom glass

Adds an **independent, default-OFF** switch for Zia 2.118's "The pop-up is see-through, the page blurred behind" appearance:
- **When enabled:** HDR Guard's existing configurable background colour, transparency and blur radius apply to the open Zia URL/search pop-up and its floating mode.
- **When disabled:** Zia's native popup appearance remains unchanged. No HDR/SDR sidebar settings are changed.
- **Over video:** Only confirmed SDR with its custom-glass option enabled or confirmed HDR with its custom-glass option enabled can use custom popup styling. Unknown video content and video cases without the corresponding opt-in stay Zia native.
- **Zia's upstream preference remains authoritative:** zia.urlbar.pop-up-blur must be ON. Classic URL bar is excluded.
- **Contrast safety:** Keep Zia's solid-popup exception for opposing light/dark popup and toolbar colours in single-toolbar mode.
- **Video safety:** This is **CSS-only popup paint**. No added will-change, backface-visibility, video brightness filters, or persistent browser-compositor promotion. Zia 2.118 continues to control the video-compositor activation/release only while its overlays are open.

## Testing
Update through Sine and restart Zen. Enable Zia's popup-glass option, then enable the new HDR Guard popup-glass checkbox. Compare normal webpages, a confirmed SDR stream and a confirmed HDR stream. For HDR, verify the video is normal before opening the popup, may dim when the popup is open, and **immediately recovers after closing it**. Check site-coloured/light popups for legibility. Hardware HDR compositing can only be confirmed on macOS/OLED.
