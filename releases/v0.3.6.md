# Zen Compact Transparent HDR Guard v0.3.6 — YouTube compositor stability experiment

## Changes

- On YouTube video URLs only, when the macOS floating sidebar is visible, avoid redundant Gecko compositor promotions of *non-selected* browser panes. Zia can promote all browser panes, then change this on video detection; HG already keeps the selected pane promoted. The new rule de-promotes hidden panes throughout this transition.
- Mark YouTube video URLs synchronously via the existing tab-selection/navigation listener, with no new timer or actor. Bilibili, unrelated websites, split views and a selected Glance overlay do not match.
- Keep v0.3.5 sidebar underlay, custom HG blur (including 10px), opacity, colours, first-frame appearance, card geometry/animation, URL popups and PiP untouched. This change does **not** modify the YouTube page DOM or its enlargement animation.

## SDR/HDR and Zia compatibility

- The mitigation requires Unified HG and Zia compact-sidebar blur on macOS. It never demotes the selected pane, promotes another pane or forces video brightness filters. HDR video may become dim while the translucent sidebar is open and must recover after it closes. SDR/HDR stream classification, the Bilibili immediate path and Glance options are unchanged.
- **Experimental hypothesis, not confirmed hardware repair:** v0.3.5 video shows sidebar dimming around two player black-frame transitions; a real transparency effect inevitably reflects changing page pixels. The compositor may also reconfigure around playback startup. This rule can reduce offscreen compositor churn, not remove actual black video pixels from the backdrop.

## Validation and hardware limitations

- GitHub CI checks all Node.js regressions, release-policy consistency, syntax and deterministic benchmarks. New regression tests cover YouTube-only synchronous markers, Bilibili isolation, split/Glance exclusions, hidden-only demotion and absence of new timers/paint changes.
- **macOS OLED SDR/HDR behavior is not verified on actual hardware.** Compare the same YouTube startup/black-frame transitions as the supplied v0.3.5 recording (approximately 2.1–3.5 seconds and 7.8–9.4 seconds) and check pause/resume, floating sidebar close, Glance thumbnails OFF, Bilibili and PiP. Genuine backdrop brightness shifts may remain.

## Rollback

- Switch `mod.compacttpr.unifiedhg` OFF in Sine to disable the new rule alongside other unified HG features. The separate `mod.compacttpr.glanceglass` option remains default-OFF.
- For full rollback install [v0.3.5](https://github.com/kcussiefil/Zen-Compact-Transparent-HDR-Guard/releases/tag/v0.3.5), SHA `5ad477aede8c39faa64082f0adbbfeb64d1a04d5`; backup branch: `backup/v0.3.5-before-v0.3.6`. Restart Zen after downgrading the Sine MOD.
