# Zen Compact Transparent HDR Guard v0.2.9 — Experimental Glance sidebar glass on macOS

## Changes

- Added the **default-OFF** Sine preference `mod.compacttpr.glanceglass` for an experimental fix when Zen Glance leaves the floating sidebar translucent but lacking real background blur.
- The optional glass follows the existing customizable background color, transparency and blur variables. No new script, observer, polling, or changes to Glance navigation were added.
- The fix applies only to macOS compact mode while a selected Glance overlay exists. Composition hints additionally require a visible sidebar and stop matching immediately when Glance closes.

## SDR/HDR and Zia compatibility

- Only the foreground `.zen-glance-overlay` is promoted while the floating sidebar is visible, and not when the preview browser itself is Zia-marked as a video.
- The original `.zen-glance-background` is promoted only when Zia and HDR Guard do not report video protection on the root or background browser. The workaround never deliberately promotes a protected background HDR video, and does not alter video filters or HDR/SDR classification.
- Bilibili retains its separate existing behavior. Zia's Blur over video and explicit HDR override can still leave HDR video dim or washed out on macOS. Foreground composition can affect compositor behavior even without directly promoting a video, so a successful blur alone does not prove color fidelity.

## Validation and hardware limitations

- New Node.js regression tests check the default-OFF switch, macOS/Glance/visible-sidebar selectors, foreground video exclusion, protected background video exclusion, and unchanged configurable appearance variables. Existing v0.2.8 tests have been scoped to the original v0.2.8 CSS section.
- The draft PR's GitHub CI runs structural and release-policy checks, but **actual macOS OLED HDR/SDR hardware testing was not performed**. Before release, verify Glance open/close on ordinary pages, an SDR video, a confirmed HDR video, and behavior after the floating sidebar closes.

## Rollback

- Immediate rollback: switch **OFF** the default-OFF `mod.compacttpr.glanceglass` setting in Sine; existing HDR Guard preferences and video handling stay intact.
